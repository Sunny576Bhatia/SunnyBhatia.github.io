About Me

I am a dedicated academician and researcher with a PhD from NIT Kurukshetra, bringing over 15 years of experience in teaching, research, and institutional leadership. Currently serving as Deputy Director of the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies.

My expertise spans quality assurance, ISO management systems, composite materials research, and academic administration. I have led multiple accreditation initiatives including NAAC, THE, and QS World Rankings, while maintaining an active research portfolio in polymer composites and mechanical engineering.
